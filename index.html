Fitting Room

An unofficial demo for Tiny’s Shopify theme studios

Fit your store to the right theme.

Paste a Shopify store. Fitting Room reads its public catalog and current theme, then picks the three best fits from 29 premium themes by Tiny’s four studios, each with the reason and the catch.

Or try a real store

Why this exists

  • Tiny’s four studios sell 29 premium Shopify themes to one shared audience of 700k+ merchants.
  • The current finder is a filter quiz over a handful of Pixel Union themes, and it asks for a larger screen.
  • Fitting Room reads the real store instead, then weighs all 29 themes against what it sells.

Built in an afternoon by Aadi Sharma with an agent swarm. Not affiliated with Tiny.
